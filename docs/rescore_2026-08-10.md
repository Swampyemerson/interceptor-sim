## 0. HEADLINE — the ruler was wrong, but not in the direction issue #8 says

Issue #8 states the scorer measures the camera lens, **"verified at +0.208 m above
the airframe datum (median over 1,748 ticks)"**, and that removing that lever brings
the adopted config from 3/16 to 12/16 (logged) and 16/16 (interpolated) inside the
0.35 m ram radius.

**The +0.208 m is not a camera lever arm.** It is `median(gt_cam_z − alt_m)`, and
`alt_m` is MAVSDK `relative_altitude_m` — altitude **above the takeoff point**, not
world z. Differencing a world z against a relative altitude measures the x500's
**landing-gear height**, not a camera mount. Three independent measurements agree
on the real number:

| route | value |
|---|---|
| Resolved SDF chain: `camera_link` at model+0.242 (mono_cam include pose) minus `base_link` at model+0.240 (`x500_base`'s own `<pose>0 0 .24>`) | **+0.002 m** |
| Physics: lowest x500 collisions are the landing-gear feet at base_link−0.2270; every flight's on-pad ticks read `gt_cam_z` = 0.2290 → 0.2290 − 0.2270 | **+0.0020 m** |
| On-pad `gt_cam_x/y` read **exactly (0.1200, 0.0300)** — the SDF lever to 4 dp, with the model at world (0,0) and yaw 0 | confirms the horizontal lever exactly |

**The camera sits ~2 mm above the airframe datum and 0.120 m forward of it.**
Sanity check: if the camera really sat 0.242 m above `base_link`, a parked x500
would report `gt_cam_z` = 0.227 + 0.242 = 0.469 m. It reports 0.229 m.

So the centre-to-centre correction is **almost entirely horizontal** (0.120 m aft,
0.030 m right, in body axes). Consequences:

1. **The 12/16 and 16/16 do not survive.** Corrected, the adopted AE5dash config
   (n=16, seeds 123+777) is **3/16** at logged ticks and **5/16** interpolated,
   against the published 3/16 → 12/16 → 16/16.
2. **The interpolation fix is real and stands** — it is worth ~0.03 m here (median
   0.482 → 0.424 m) and it is the honest way to score a 20 Hz sample of a 13 m/s
   flyby. It is simply much smaller than the (spurious) 0.208 m vertical drop.
3. **"The miss is vertical, −0.37 m" STANDS; it does not halve.** At the corrected
   centre-to-centre CPA the vertical component is **median −0.374 m** (range −0.457
   to −0.280) versus **−0.375 m** at the lens. The `scoring_fix_plan.md` §4 row
   predicting "~−0.16 m real vertical" is wrong: essentially none of the vertical
   offset was measurement artefact. The interceptor genuinely flies ~0.37 m above
   the target's centre, and **that — not the ruler — is what keeps the adopted
   config outside the 0.35 m ram radius.** The remaining horizontal miss at CPA is
   a median of only 0.174 m; the vertical channel is the whole gap.
4. **Do not implement `scoring_fix_plan.md` §2's "return `p_model` as base_link".**
   `tracker.latest[DRONE_MODEL]` is the **model origin**, which sits 0.240 m *below*
   `base_link` (essentially at the landing-gear datum). Scoring from it would be a
   larger error than the lens it replaces — in the flattering direction. It is
   reported below as mode `model` precisely so this is visible, not repeated.

**Framing (unchanged, and it still binds):** every number here is a perfect launch
cue (`--dash-target-err-n/e` = 0.0), co-altitude by construction, no wind, sim
optics. These are **best-case upper bounds**, not field results.

## 3. How the primary number is built, and what it is not

* **`centre` (PRIMARY)** — `p_base = p_cam − R_yaw · (0.120 fwd, 0.030 left, 0.002 up)`.
  Yaw is **calibrated per flight from the on-pad ticks**, where the true body yaw is
  known (model spawns at world (0,0) yaw 0, verified by the exact (0.12, 0.03)
  camera offset). Without that calibration the naive `heading = (sin ψ, cos ψ)`
  carries this world's ~6.2° yaw-datum offset — the pad ticks read ψ = 96.2° while
  the airframe points along world +x. Cross-checked against each flight's own
  ground-truth velocity heading in CODED_DASH: **median 6.8°, max 14.4°** over 126
  checkable flights (the residual is real dash sideslip).
* **Interpolation** — both world positions are linearly interpolated across each
  tick pair, so |Δp(s)|² is a quadratic in s ∈ [0,1] with a closed-form minimum,
  clamped to the segment. **No clock enters the computation**, so the sim-time/
  wall-time hazard does not apply. A segment whose relative displacement exceeds
  2.0 m is counted as a logging gap and reported (0 occurred).
* **The acknowledged approximation, and its measured bound.** Pitch/roll are not
  logged in the historical era, so `centre` rotates the lever by yaw only. A dashing
  quad is nose-down 5–35°, which swings the camera *below* base_link
  (`0.002·cos d − 0.120·sin d`). Mode `centre-tilt` recovers the tilt per tick from
  `alt_m` against the per-flight pad calibration and re-rotates the lever; the two
  are always reported and their per-arm worst-flight difference is the
  **`recon bound` column: 0.000–0.076 m, median arm 0.034 m.** That bound is
  measured on the same flights, not asserted. It is the reason the conservative
  logged-tick column rides alongside permanently.
* **Not corrected:** roll during a hard lateral strafe (the tilt solver attributes
  it to pitch; bounded by the 0.030 m lateral lever, ≤0.015 m), and the ~3 cm
  between `base_link` and the rotor-plane centroid (base_link is the horizontal
  geometric centre exactly — rotors at ±0.174, ±0.174 — and 0.044 m below the prop
  disc). Neither is inside the reconstruction bound above; both are smaller.
* **Era.** All 21 arms flew world `quad_enemy` (auto-detected from each arm's own
  run log), i.e. the `fpv_quad_enemy` target whose model origin **is** its airframe
  centre (single static link, `base_link_visual` at +0.025). **No arm in this sweep
  needs the AprilTag/billboard-era caveat.** The tool still prints the caveat for
  any arm whose world is not on the verified list rather than assuming it.
* **The radius did not move.** `RAM_RADIUS_M = 0.35` (ADR-0084) is hardcoded with
  no CLI flag; a test asserts there is no way to tune it.

## 4. What this changes for the plan (`docs/scoring_fix_plan.md`)

| plan says | should now say |
|---|---|
| §2 #8: "`ground_truth_world_points()` already holds the interceptor **base_link** (`p_model`) … return it too" | `p_model` is the **model origin, 0.240 m below base_link**. The correct additive column is `p_model + 0.240·(body up)`, or better, log the attitude quaternion and compute it downstream. |
| §4: "the miss is vertical, −0.37 m" → "~−0.16 m real vertical after removing the 0.208 m lens lever" | **Wrong. The vertical miss is −0.374 m at the airframe centre.** Nothing to remove. |
| §4: "0/16 … becomes 12/16 logged, 16/16 interpolated" | **3/16 logged, 5/16 interpolated.** |
| §4: "the terminal must earn ~1 m" → "near zero for the adopted config" | The adopted config's median corrected CPA is 0.424 m against a 0.35 m radius: **~0.07 m still to close at the median, 11/16 flights outside**, under a perfect cue — and the gap is vertical (median −0.374 m vertical vs 0.174 m horizontal), so it is an altitude/vertical-channel problem, not a terminal-lateral-accuracy problem. |
| §6 risk 6b: the wind/error-correction workflow "may be correcting a number that is 56% measurement artefact" | **It is not.** The −0.37 m vertical is real; that workflow's sizing stands on this point. |

Not done here (deliberately — file ownership): no edit to `m4_intercept.py`, no
edit to `project_state.json`/dashboard, no ADR, no commit.
